# DMI Platform Roadmap

> Enterprise Financial Analysis Platform for Vietnam Stock Market

---

# Vision

Build a complete enterprise investment platform for Vietnam financial markets.

The platform will provide:

- Financial Data Platform
- Financial Metrics
- Company Valuation
- Stock Screening
- Portfolio Management
- Market Analytics
- AI Investment Assistant
